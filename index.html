FAQ

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship from our partner warehouse within 1–3 business days. Delivery usually takes 7–15 business days to the US, UK, EU and UAE. You get a tracking number by email.

How do I pay?

Add items to your cart, pick a network (TRC-20, BEP-20 or TON), send the exact USDT amount to the address shown at checkout, then paste your transaction ID in the order form. We confirm your order by email within 24 hours.

What if something arrives damaged or I'm not happy?

Email us within 30 days of delivery with a photo and we'll send a replacement or refund your USDT to the wallet you paid from.

Is the steel seed plate safe?

It's a passive stainless-steel plate with no electronics and no connectivity. You stamp or engrave your words yourself. We never see your seed phrase and never ask for it.
